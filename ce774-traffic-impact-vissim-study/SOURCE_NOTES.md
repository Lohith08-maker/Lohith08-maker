## Quality checks

1. Vehicle counts sum to **180**, and 16 / 180 = **8.89%** (rounded), which supports the cited HCV share.
2. Speed-frequency bin counts sum to **180**, but there is no individual record-level speed dataset.
3. Conflicting reported mean HCV speeds: **25 km/h** (slide 6) and **23 km/h** (slide 9). Do not report a single precise average without reconciling original records.
4. No credible causal estimate that HCVs alone *caused* a particular percentage of delays was provided. Avoid overclaiming.
5. The project says the team used PTV VISSIM, but published material does not establish who personally operated it. The README deliberately describes simulation as group work.
