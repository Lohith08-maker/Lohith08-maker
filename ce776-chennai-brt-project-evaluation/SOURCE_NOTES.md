## Checking arithmetic

- Slide 2: ₹1,006.73 Cr + ₹1,147.54 Cr = ₹2,154.27 Cr, **not ₹2,132.9 Cr**.
- Slide 10 gives 25 minutes, ₹120/hour, 40 passengers, 5,450 trips/day. Direct multiplication yields **₹109 lakh/day**; slide reports **₹10.91 lakh/day**. The time-savings figures should not be quoted as independently verified benefits.
- Slide 9 base BCR 2.14; slide 14 BCR 1.67. They might refer to different methods or cases, but this isn't explained in the deck.
- Slide 5 references 12% discount rate; slide 8 10%. Slide 5 and 8 also mention differing capital-cost bases.
- Slide 12 lists MCDA criteria but does not show weights or a numerical ranking, so claim **contributed to an MCDA framework**, not "validated optimal corridor with a scored MCDA."

## Research integrity

These records represent a **student team presentation**. Reported financial numbers and scenarios are *not independent evidence that the Chennai BRTS achieved any stated benefit*. The student's individual role is self-reported as secondary research, user-cost/time-savings analysis, MCDA, recommendations, and presentation; no claim of being principal author of the full financial model.

Neither the team's original unredacted deck nor classmates' personal identifiers are published.
